A survey of spectral variability detecting and classifying types of variability based on observations using the Murchison Widefield Array. Spectral information was able to distinguish between physical origins of the variability, including scintillation due to the interstellar medium (twinkling) or changes in the opitcal depth (likely due to clumpy frustrated environments surrounding the galaxies).
