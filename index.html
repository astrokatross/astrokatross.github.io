Astrophysicist · Science Communicator · Activist

I watch galaxies twinkling and help make science a welcoming environment for everyone.

I am an astrophysicist based at the Australian SKA Regional Centre (AusSRC) at Curtin University in Boorloo (Perth). As a support scientist, I work to prepare Australia for the next generation of radio telescopes, making sure we have the computers and expertise needed to be able to explore the Universe in more detail than ever before.

I am an astrophysicist studying active galactic nuclei (AGN) and baby black holes using the radio rainbow. I use world-class telescopes, like the Murchison Widefield Array (MWA) to piece together galaxy evolution and study the Universe in "radio colour". I have worked extensively as a science communicator and activist for inclusive STEM spaces including founding IncludeHer, an international organisation that aims for widespread science education reform to shift dominant about science and scientists. I am a proud bisexual and agender person and was diagnosed with ADHD as an adult.
